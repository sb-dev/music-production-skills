# Northbound

**Level 1 — indie folk — controlled output**

Develop an acoustic refrain and small-form arrangement using the lowest useful production resolution.

## Prompt

```text
Use music-compose to create an 80-second indie-folk demo called "Northbound".

The piece should feel grounded, reflective and human, built around a refrain that could plausibly be sung with only an acoustic guitar.

Requirements:
- Duration: about 80 seconds
- Genre: indie folk / acoustic singer-songwriter
- Tempo: around 86 BPM
- Meter: 4/4
- Palette: fingerpicked acoustic guitar, light upright or electric bass, very restrained percussion
- Vocal: one natural lead voice
- Lyric subject: leaving a familiar place without knowing whether returning is the right choice
- Structure: verse → refrain → short second verse or instrumental turn → refrain

Workflow:
- Write the refrain lyric and melody before expanding the arrangement
- Validate chords, melody range and phrasing using text/MIDI or a simple voice-and-guitar representation
- Keep instrumental additions subordinate to the song
- Create one concise demo once the refrain and structure are stable
- Evaluate song strength separately from recording polish

What to optimise for:
- singable melody
- specific but economical lyric writing
- a refrain that resolves emotionally without sounding conclusive
- natural acoustic pacing
- arrangement restraint
```

## What this example tests

- song-first composition
- cheap acoustic representation before production
- separation of songwriting quality from recording quality
