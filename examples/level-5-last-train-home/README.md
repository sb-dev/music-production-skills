# Last Train Home

**Level 5 — ambient electronic — video handoff**

Create an understated electronic track around supplied picture/editorial timing while keeping Video and Music responsibilities separate.

## Prompt

```text
Use music-compose, music-produce and music-evaluate to create a 45-second ambient-electronic track called "Last Train Home" for an existing video edit.

Upstream video artifacts provide:
- locked edit duration: 45 seconds
- key transition points at 00:12, 00:27 and 00:38
- dialogue occupies 00:05–00:11 and 00:29–00:35
- final visual resolves quietly rather than triumphantly

Music requirements:
- Genre: ambient electronic / minimal pulse
- Instrumental
- No dominant lead during dialogue windows
- Build subtle motion across the piece without turning into trailer music
- One final harmonic or textural resolution should align with the last picture beat

Workflow:
- Consume the supplied edit timing; do not redefine video editorial decisions
- Convert picture constraints into a music brief and arrangement
- Use a low-cost mock/draft to test pacing before final production
- Produce the approved cue/track
- Ensure dialogue windows have enough musical space
- Export timing metadata showing structural points useful to Video Production Skills
- Evaluate musical coherence and picture-support constraints separately from video quality

Deliverables:
- 45-second approved music master
- timing map / structural markers
- optional alternative under-dialogue balance only if supported by the production source

What to optimise for:
- timing relationship to the locked edit
- restraint under dialogue
- continuity across picture transitions
- a quiet but intentional final resolution
- clean Video ↔ Music artifact boundary
```

## What this example tests

- video edit → music brief handoff
- timing-aware production without absorbing video editorial
- timing-map delivery
