# After Hours

**Level 5 — house — advertising handoff**

Turn an advertising brief into a coherent house track plus delivery variants without letting campaign strategy leak into music-domain ownership.

## Prompt

```text
Use music-compose, music-produce and music-evaluate to create a house music package called "After Hours" from an supplied advertising brief.

Upstream campaign constraints:
- Product: premium urban night-time event brand
- Audience: adults who already know club culture; avoid generic festival bombast
- Desired feeling: anticipation, movement, controlled confidence
- Required video uses: 60-second hero film, 15-second social cut, 6-second bumper

Music requirements:
- Genre: deep / modern house
- Tempo: 124 BPM
- Instrumental
- Strong rhythmic identity within the first few seconds
- One recognisable musical motif that survives every delivery length

Workflow:
- Treat audience, proposition and campaign intent as supplied constraints; do not rewrite campaign strategy
- Create and approve the music brief and core composition
- Produce the full track first
- Derive delivery variants from the approved musical identity rather than composing unrelated tracks for each duration
- Create timing metadata that identifies strong edit points and motif entries for the video team
- Provide genuine stems only if the production source supports them
- Evaluate each variant for musical coherence at its actual duration

Deliverables:
- approved full master
- 60-second hero edit
- 15-second social edit
- 6-second bumper
- timing/edit-point metadata
- optional genuine stems where available

What to optimise for:
- immediate rhythmic identity
- consistent motif across variants
- edits that feel musical rather than merely truncated
- clean artifact handoff to advertising/video production
```

## What this example tests

- advertising → music artifact handoff
- one musical identity across delivery variants
- delivery_package and timing metadata
