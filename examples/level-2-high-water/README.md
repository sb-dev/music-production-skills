# High Water

**Level 2 — blues / roots rock — selection and preservation**

Compare rhythmic and melodic interpretations of the same roots song before locking the performance direction.

## Prompt

```text
Use music-compose, music-produce and music-evaluate to develop a roots-rock / modern blues demo called "High Water".

Requirements:
- Tempo: around 96 BPM
- Meter: 4/4
- Palette: electric guitar, bass, live-feel drums, optional organ
- Vocal: direct, slightly rough lead voice
- Song subject: seeing a familiar town change after years away
- Keep the core verse lyric and basic harmonic movement stable

Create three refrain directions:
- A: straight, strong melodic refrain over a restrained groove
- B: syncopated refrain with a stop-start band response
- C: call-and-response between lead vocal and guitar

Workflow:
- Use simple chord, rhythm and melody representations before full-band generation
- Compare memorability, singability, groove and how naturally each refrain grows from the verse
- Select the strongest refrain and rhythm feel
- Preserve the approved lyric and harmonic movement while refining the selected direction
- Produce a concise demo and verify that no unapproved lyric or structural changes were introduced

What to optimise for:
- believable band interplay
- a refrain that sounds earned rather than pasted on
- groove consistency
- vocal phrasing that feels playable/singable
- preservation of approved song decisions
```

## What this example tests

- alternative refrain semantics
- performance-direction selection
- approved lyric/harmony preservation
