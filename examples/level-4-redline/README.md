# Redline

**Level 4 — drum & bass — diagnose and repair**

Diagnose why one drop underperforms, route the defect to the owning production layer, and repair only that section.

## Prompt

```text
Use music-evaluate and music-produce to diagnose and repair an existing drum-and-bass track called "Redline".

Given:
- Tempo: 174 BPM
- Genre: liquid / melodic drum & bass
- The intro, first drop and breakdown are approved
- The second drop from roughly 01:42–02:08 feels noticeably weaker than the first
- Melody, chord progression, overall structure and first-drop production are locked

Task:
- Evaluate the second drop against the first and against the original brief
- Determine whether the weakness belongs primarily to arrangement, production, performance/generation or technical mix balance
- Do not recommend "make it louder" unless evidence shows level is actually the cause
- Identify the smallest musical/production unit that needs to change
- Repair only the second drop where the execution path permits local refinement
- Preserve the locked melody, harmony, structure and first drop
- Re-evaluate the repaired region and its entry/exit boundaries

What to optimise for:
- correct failure routing
- restored drop impact without rewriting the song
- bass/drum relationship
- continuity into and out of the repaired region
- explicit preservation evidence
```

## What this example tests

- failure classification before repair
- section-local correction
- regression and boundary evaluation
