# Sunday Static

**Level 1 — neo-soul / R&B — controlled output**

Build a compact vocal-song sketch where harmony, groove and topline work together before committing to full production.

## Prompt

```text
Use music-compose to create a 70-second neo-soul / R&B song sketch called "Sunday Static".

The song should feel intimate, warm and conversational, like a quiet late-morning record rather than a dramatic power ballad.

Requirements:
- Duration: about 70 seconds
- Genre: neo-soul / contemporary R&B
- Tempo: 74 BPM
- Meter: 4/4 with a relaxed pocket
- Palette: Rhodes-style keys, round electric bass, dry drums, restrained guitar accents
- Vocal: one lead voice with a short memorable refrain
- Lyrics: simple and specific; avoid grand declarations or dense storytelling
- Structure: short intro → verse idea → refrain → brief outro

Workflow:
- Establish the harmonic loop and groove before polishing lyrics
- Draft a concise topline that fits the natural stress of the lyric
- Keep the arrangement sparse enough that the vocal remains the centre
- Use MIDI or structured chord/rhythm descriptions before generating audio where possible
- Produce only a short demo sufficient to judge harmony, pocket and topline together
- Run music-evaluate and route any weakness back to composition or arrangement rather than hiding it with production polish

What to optimise for:
- natural vocal phrasing
- harmonic colour without unnecessary chord complexity
- bass/drum pocket
- a refrain that feels inevitable rather than oversized
- space in the arrangement
```

## What this example tests

- brief → song sketch → short demo
- vocal, harmony and groove integration
- composition problems routed upstream rather than masked by production
