# Half Light

**Level 3 — alternative R&B — full production**

Produce a complete vocal track while protecting lyric, topline and song structure through the higher-fidelity stages.

## Prompt

```text
Use music-compose, music-produce and music-evaluate to create a finished alternative-R&B song called "Half Light".

Requirements:
- Duration: 2:30–3:00
- Tempo: 78 BPM
- Mood: intimate, unresolved, nocturnal
- Palette: soft keys, sub bass, clipped drums, sparse guitar or synth texture
- Vocal: one close lead voice; restrained doubles or harmonies only where they add meaning
- Lyric theme: two people delaying a conversation they both know is coming
- Structure: verse → pre-chorus → chorus → verse → chorus → short bridge → final chorus

Workflow:
- Develop and approve lyrics, topline, harmony and structure before final production
- Use a demo to confirm that the vocal sits naturally against the pocket
- Lock the approved lyric and core melody before production
- Choose a compatible Replicate music model based on current requirements
- Produce the full track, preserving locked words and melody as far as the execution method permits
- If a local vocal or production defect appears, repair that region rather than restarting the song
- Evaluate performance/generation artefacts separately from songwriting defects
- Run technical QC and final evaluation against the original brief

What to optimise for:
- intelligible, natural vocal phrasing
- restrained production around the lead
- chorus lift created by arrangement rather than loudness alone
- preservation of exact approved lyric content
- consistent vocal identity across sections
```

## What this example tests

- vocal-song full production
- locked lyric/topline preservation
- local repair of generated vocal/production defects
